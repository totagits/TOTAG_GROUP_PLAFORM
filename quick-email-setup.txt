TOTAG GROUP EMAIL SETUP - QUICK REFERENCE

STEP 1: Sign up at zoho.com/mail (FREE)
STEP 2: Add domain: totaggroup.com
STEP 3: Add these DNS records in Name.com:

MX RECORDS:
Type: MX, Name: @, Value: mx.zoho.com, Priority: 10
Type: MX, Name: @, Value: mx2.zoho.com, Priority: 20  
Type: MX, Name: @, Value: mx3.zoho.com, Priority: 50

TXT RECORD:
Type: TXT, Name: @, Value: v=spf1 include:zoho.com ~all

STEP 4: Wait 24-48 hours for DNS propagation

- admin@totaggroup.com
