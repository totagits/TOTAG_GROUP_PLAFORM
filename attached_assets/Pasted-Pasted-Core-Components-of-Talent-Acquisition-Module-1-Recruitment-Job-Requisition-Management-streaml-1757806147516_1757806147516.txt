Core Components of Talent Acquisition Module

1. Recruitment

Job Requisition Management → streamlined approval workflows for new roles.

Job Posting & Distribution → multi-channel (company site, job boards, LinkedIn, social media).

Applicant Tracking System (ATS) → candidate applications, statuses, and communication tracking.

Resume Parsing & Screening → AI-based keyword and skills extraction.

Candidate Relationship Management (CRM) → talent pools, talent pipelines, and engagement campaigns.

Interview Management → scheduling, panel coordination, structured interview guides.

Assessment Integration → technical tests, psychometric assessments, and video interviews.

Offer Management → automated offer letters, e-signatures, and background checks.


2. Onboarding

Pre-boarding → digital welcome kits, paperwork, policy acknowledgment before Day 1.

Paperless Onboarding → contracts, benefits enrollment, ID verification online.

Task & Workflow Automation → checklists for IT setup, compliance, and orientation.

Buddy/Mentor Assignment → cultural integration support.

Learning Integration → mandatory training, compliance courses, and role-based learning.

First-90-Days Journey → milestone tracking, pulse surveys, and manager check-ins.



---

🚀 Best Practices

Candidate Experience First → smooth, transparent, mobile-friendly application process.

Employer Branding → careers portal with videos, testimonials, and value propositions.

Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI) → bias-free AI tools, diverse sourcing channels.

Data-Driven Hiring → track time-to-hire, cost-per-hire, quality-of-hire.

Automation with Human Touch → chatbots for FAQs, recruiters for relationship-building.

Seamless Transition to Core HR → onboarding data flows directly into employee master records.

Feedback Loops → candidate experience surveys, new hire feedback.
