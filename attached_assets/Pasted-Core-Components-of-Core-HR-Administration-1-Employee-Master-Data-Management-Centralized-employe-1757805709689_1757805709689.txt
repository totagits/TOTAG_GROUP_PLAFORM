Core Components of Core HR & Administration

1. Employee Master Data Management

Centralized employee records (personal info, job, contract, compensation, skills).

Digital document management (contracts, IDs, certifications).

Secure employee lifecycle tracking (hire → transfer → promotion → exit).



2. Organizational Management

Org structure visualization (departments, reporting lines).

Position and job management.

Multi-entity and multi-location setup.

Workforce planning capabilities.



3. HR Administration

Employment contracts and policy acknowledgment tracking.

Leave and attendance policy setup.

Benefits and entitlement administration.

Disciplinary actions and grievance tracking.



4. Workforce Lifecycle Management

Onboarding/offboarding processes.

Transfers, promotions, role changes.

Exit management and final settlement tracking.



5. Self-Service Integration

Employee & Manager Self-Service (ESS/MSS) hooks.

Policy library and HR knowledge base.

Requests and approvals workflow (letters, IDs, verification).





---

🚀 Best Practices

Single Source of Truth → Core HR should be the master repository of employee and organizational data.

Automation-First → Minimize manual processes with automated workflows.

Compliance Embedded → Local labor laws, contracts, and policy compliance tracked digitally.

Employee-Centric → User-friendly portals and mobile-first access.

Integration Ready → Serve as the backbone for Payroll, Talent, L&D, and Analytics.

Data Security → Strict role-based access control, GDPR compliance, audit trails.

Scalability → Flexible to handle growth, mergers, or restructuring.
